\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

This manuscript currently describes a benchmark design, not a completed
dataset or empirical finding. The proposed core tasks and relation inventory
still require annotation pilots. In particular, some role assignments may
be recoverable from cue names alone, and some cultural associations may
require information absent from the video.

Online discovery favors visible, well-documented, and frequently uploaded
practices. Multilingual and model-assisted search can broaden retrieval,
but cannot establish representative cultural coverage. Our working
definition also favors identifiable cues; cultural meaning can be implicit,
contested, or shared across communities. A single set of labels cannot
capture all valid interpretations.

Natural recordings do not provide fully controlled interventions on
cultural context. Model comparisons can also be affected by unequal input
budgets, access to audio, and unknown training data. These constraints must
be reported with any later conclusions.
