\section*{Ethical Considerations}
\label{sec:ethics}

Cultural labels describe supported associations of particular elements.
They should not be used to rank cultures, decide authenticity, or infer a
person's nationality or ethnicity from appearance. A practice performed
abroad may involve several communities; neither location nor a participant's
appearance establishes that affiliation. Cases requiring contextual records
must be identified as such.

Collection and release should respect source rights and the interests of
people depicted. Public visibility is not equivalent to consent for every
reuse. Annotators should have a way to flag private, sacred, restricted, or
misrepresented practices, and to record disagreement without being forced
to select one national label. The benchmark should explain its coverage
limits and provide a correction or removal process.
